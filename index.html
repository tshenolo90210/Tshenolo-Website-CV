About

I've always liked figuring out how things work — a piece of code, a technical drawing, a building going up. I'm taking 2026 as a gap year, and I've used it to go back and upgrade my Maths and Physical Science marks so I'm properly ready for a career in IT and engineering.

I've also been getting as much real-world experience as I can. I've worked on Stefanutti Stocks construction sites, spent three months in marketing and finance at the Insurance Institute of South Africa (and was named Employee of the Month in March), and I'm now working in sales at Everon. Each one showed me a different side of how work actually gets done.

Sport has been a big part of my life too. At Fourways High I ran cross country and middle distance, did orienteering, played rugby, and was vice-captain of the volleyball second team — which is where I learned to make a call and own it. Now I'm looking for internships, job shadowing or work in Gauteng: somewhere I can keep learning, ask good questions and pull my weight.
